Scientific & Geotechnical Formulations

Strict distinction between physical mechanics (FoS) and empirical/probabilistic classifiers (CRI & Event Model).

1. Mohr-Coulomb Stability (FoS) PHYSICAL MODEL
FoS = [ c' + (γ·z·cos²β - u)·tan φ' ] / [ γ·z·sin β·cos β ]

Infinite-slope equilibrium where c' is effective cohesion, φ' is friction angle, γ is unit soil weight, z is slip depth, β is slope angle, and u is piezometer pore-water pressure. Values below 1.0 indicate physical slope failure.

2. Composite Risk Index (CRI) FUSION METRIC
H = α·S + β·P + γ·A
CRI = H × V × 100

Harmonizes intrinsic slope susceptibility (S), real-time triggering precipitation (P), and anthropogenic toe-cutting (A) scaled by vulnerability exposure (V). CRI represents regional operational severity on a 0–100 scale.

3. Mandal-Sarkar Rainfall I-D HYDRO METEOROLOGY
I_threshold = 1.48 · D^(-0.42)
API_30d = Σ (0.84^t · R_t)

Established empirical rainfall Intensity-Duration threshold for the Sikkim-Darjeeling Himalayas (Mandal & Sarkar, 2021). Evaluates both hourly downpour bursts (I) and 30-day antecedent soil saturation memory (API).
