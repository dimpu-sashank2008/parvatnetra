## 1. Executive Defense Strategy & Core Architectural Invariants

PARVAT NETRA's defense against technical national-level judges is founded upon **absolute scientific honesty**, **rigid statutory safety**, and **uncompromising architectural transparency**:

1. **Target Distinction**: Factor of Safety ($FoS$, physical Newton-Euler limit equilibrium) is never confused with empirical event probability ($P(event)$, calibrated Gradient Boosting Classifier). They are reported as distinct outputs alongside the multi-criteria Composite Risk Index ($CRI$).
2. **Temporal Model Honesty**: The recurrent LSTM model is openly designated `NOT_TRAINED / PHYSICS-INFORMED TEMPORAL SURROGATE`. We strictly refused to train on 17 discrete disaster snapshots, enforcing the `DATA_COLLECTION_REQUIRED` execution guard.
3. **Small Sample Disclosure**: We openly disclose that the operational event classifier is trained on $N=17$ authenticated historical events and evaluated on an $N=8$ temporal holdout test set. We describe it as a *data-grounded research prototype*, completely rejecting claims of "100% production accuracy".
4. **Non-Causal Physics Grammar**: The system never asserts that rain "caused" a failure. It explains covariation: *"risk increased alongside higher hydrological loading and lower computed FoS"*.
5. **Human-in-the-Loop Statutory Interlocks**: AI recommendations are strictly advisory. Production emergency broadcasts and sirens are disarmed (`ENABLE_PUBLIC_DISPATCH=0`, `SIREN_DRY_RUN=1`). Actuation voice commands are intercepted and rejected under Section 34(c) of the Disaster Management Act 2005 (DMA 2005).
