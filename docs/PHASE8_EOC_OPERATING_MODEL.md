## 2. Role Specifications, Permissions & Constraints

### 2.1. PUBLIC (Civilian / Tourist / Citizen)
- **Profile**: Unauthenticated or self-registered citizen, tourist, commercial driver, or community volunteer within the Himalayan pilot corridor.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - View public advisory hazard maps, designated evacuation shelters, and safe mountain corridors.
  - Subscribe to geo-fenced early-warning notifications (Web Push, Mobile Push, SMS) with explicit consent.
  - Submit crowd-sourced field observations (tension cracks, road debris, localized pooling) with photographic and GPS evidence.
  - Submit safety acknowledgement status (`SAFE`, `NEED_ASSISTANCE`, `EVACUATING`).
- **Permissions**:
  - `READ_PUBLIC_MAP`: Allowed.
  - `SUBSCRIBE_NOTIFICATIONS`: Allowed (with consent).
  - `SUBMIT_CITIZEN_REPORT`: Allowed.
  - `SUBMIT_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT`: Allowed.
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Cannot access internal sensor telemetry, raw piezometer curves, or unverified AI risk recommendations.
  - Cannot view pending authority dossiers or internal EOC triage queues.
  - Cannot trigger sirens, modify road status, or authorize public alerts.

### 2.2. FIELD_OPERATOR (BRO Quick Reaction Unit / SDRF Ground Team)
- **Profile**: Border Roads Organisation (BRO) engineer, State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) responder, or field telemetry technician.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Receive field inspection taskings generated by EOC triage.
  - Navigate to suspicious slopes using multi-profile offline navigation (`FASTEST`, `SHORTEST`, `SAFEST`).
  - Conduct ground-truth validation: measure tension crack aperture, inspect road crown subsidence, check culvert clogging.
  - Submit cryptographically signed field verification reports with geotagged media, slope deformation status, and road blockages.
- **Permissions**:
  - `READ_OPERATIONAL_TELEMETRY`: Allowed.
  - `ACCEPT_FIELD_TASK`: Allowed.
  - `SUBMIT_FIELD_VERIFICATION`: Allowed.
  - `REQUEST_EOC_ESCALATION`: Allowed.
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Cannot unilaterally authorize public emergency broadcast.
  - Cannot silence active physical corridor sirens without EOC authorization.
  - Cannot modify corridor geotechnical baseline parameters.

### 2.3. EOC_OPERATOR (EOC Watchstander / Multi-Source Triage Specialist)
- **Profile**: 24/7 watchstander at the District or State Emergency Operations Center.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Monitor continuous live telemetry, IMD nowcasts, CWC Teesta hydro-telemetry, and PAHAD AI predictions.
  - Triage anomaly events and evaluate multi-source corroboration status (2-of-3 signal rule).
  - Convert corroborated PAHAD risk recommendations into formal EOC incidents.
  - Dispatch ground inspection tasks to BRO/SDRF field units.
  - Prepare authority dossiers and submit incident for statutory review.
  - Monitor multi-channel delivery states and recipient acknowledgements.
- **Permissions**:
  - `TRIAGE_INCIDENT`: Allowed.
  - `DISPATCH_FIELD_TASK`: Allowed.
  - `PREPARE_AUTHORITY_DOSSIER`: Allowed.
  - `TRIGGER_SIREN_DRY_RUN`: Allowed (audit-logged).
  - `PREPARE_CAP_DRAFT`: Allowed.
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Cannot authorize public emergency broadcast without District or State Authority statutory signature.
  - Cannot bypass the 2-of-3 corroboration gate.
  - Cannot delete audit logs or override tamper-evident hash chains.

### 2.4. DISTRICT_AUTHORITY (District Magistrate / DDMA Director / Collector)
- **Profile**: Statutory executive disaster authority for the district (e.g., Pakyong / Gangtok / Kalimpong).
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Review complete 18-point incident dossier, including physical FoS, IMD precipitation, ML probability, InSAR velocity, and BRO field verification reports.
  - Authorize public emergency broadcast within the 15 km geodesic safety geofence.
  - Issue executive road closures (e.g., NH-10 KM 48 29th Mile closure) and direct diversion to NH-717A.
  - Authorize All-Clear and incident stand-down following corroborated stabilization.
- **Permissions**:
  - `AUTHORIZE_DISTRICT_ALERT`: Allowed.
  - `REJECT_ALERT`: Allowed (requires mandatory statutory justification).
  - `AUTHORIZE_ROAD_CLOSURE`: Allowed.
  - `AUTHORIZE_ALL_CLEAR`: Allowed.
  - `INITIATE_WITHDRAWAL`: Allowed.
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Cannot approve statewide evacuations outside jurisdictional district boundary.
  - Cannot authorize public alerts when 2-of-3 corroboration is lacking, except under documented statutory emergency override with NDMA audit notice.

### 2.5. STATE_AUTHORITY (SDMA Relief Commissioner / BRO Project Commander)
- **Profile**: State-level executive authority (Sikkim / West Bengal Disaster Management Authority, BRO Swastik/Dantak).
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Inter-district corridor coordination (e.g., Siliguri-to-Gangtok supply chain continuity).
  - Approve multi-district evacuation directives, statewide CAP v1.2 dissemination, and strategic army/relief staging.
  - Resolve escalated incidents where district response windows have expired.
- **Permissions**:
  - `AUTHORIZE_STATE_ALERT`: Allowed.
  - `OVERRIDE_DISTRICT_ACTION`: Allowed.
  - `MOBILIZE_STATE_RESERVES`: Allowed.
  - `AUTHORIZE_BRO_STRATEGIC_DETOUR`: Allowed.
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Cannot disable tamper-evident SHA-256 system audit logging.
  - Cannot delete field evidence or retrospective model validation data.

### 2.6. ADMIN (National Disaster Intelligence / System Administrator)
- **Profile**: Technical administrator, NDMA IT director, system architect.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - System configuration, credential rotation, hardware relay pairing, and model versioning governance.
  - Monitor health of database instances, API backhauls, edge mesh networks, and SMS gateways.
  - Perform scheduled fail-closed drills, chaos testing, and after-action review compilation.
- **Permissions**:
  - `MANAGE_SYSTEM_CONFIG`: Allowed.
  - `ROTATE_SECURITY_KEYS`: Allowed.
  - `MANAGE_MODEL_REGISTRY`: Allowed.
  - `RUN_RESILIENCE_DRILLS`: Allowed.
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Cannot directly issue civilian evacuation orders (statutory separation of engineering vs administrative executive power).

## 3. Statutory Approval & Emergency Override Authority

| Action | Required Role | Required Corroboration | Secondary Approver | Fail-Closed Timeout |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Field Ground Tasking** | `EOC_OPERATOR` | 1 Signal (Pore Pressure / Anomaly) | None | 15 minutes |
| **District Emergency Alert** | `DISTRICT_AUTHORITY` | 2-of-3 Mandatory ($FoS < 1.10$, $\text{Rain} > 150\,\text{mm}$, $\text{ML} > 0.70$) | `EOC_OPERATOR` (dossier prep) | 30 minutes $\to$ Escalation |
| **Corridor Road Closure (NH-10)** | `DISTRICT_AUTHORITY` / BRO | Corroborated risk or verified debris | District Police / BRO OC | 20 minutes |
| **Statewide CAP v1.2 Broadcast** | `STATE_AUTHORITY` | 2-of-3 Mandatory + Inter-district impact | `DISTRICT_AUTHORITY` | 45 minutes $\to$ State EOC alert |
| **Acoustic Corridor Siren (DRY_RUN)** | `EOC_OPERATOR` | Corroboration + Test token | None | Immediate |
| **Acoustic Corridor Siren (LIVE)** | `DISTRICT_AUTHORITY` | 2-of-3 Mandatory + Confirmed failure | State EOC | 10 minutes |
| **Incident All-Clear & Stand-Down** | `DISTRICT_AUTHORITY` | Decreased risk + Field confirmation + Authority review | BRO OC Ground Confirmation | 60 minutes |
| **False-Alarm Retraction Broadcast** | `DISTRICT_AUTHORITY` | Contradictory evidence + Ground truth | EOC Incident Commander | 15 minutes |

## 4. Prohibited System Behaviors & Red Lines

1. **NO Autonomous Civilian Broadcast**: No AI algorithm, mathematical model, or threshold trigger may dispatch a civilian notification, SMS, or siren activation without statutory human authorization.
2. **NO Single-Sensor Triggering**: A single sensor spike (e.g., piezometer glitch) CANNOT trigger an alert candidate without secondary confirmation.
3. **NO Silent Timeout Approval**: Expiration of an authority review window MUST escalate to a higher authority tier or prompt watchstanders; it must NEVER default to auto-approval.
4. **NO Fabricated Deliveries**: Simulated SMS or test push events must NEVER be recorded as `DELIVERED` without carrier/device delivery receipts.
5. **NO Unsigned Siren Trigger**: Corridor sirens require an unexpired, cryptographically signed token with HMAC-SHA256 signature and unique nonce to prevent replay attacks.
