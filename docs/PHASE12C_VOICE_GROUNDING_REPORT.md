## 1. Safety Architecture & Actuation Rejection
The PARVAT NETRA Voice Assistant provides conversational intelligence for Emergency Operation Centre (EOC) operators, district officials, and field responders. 

### Mandatory Safety Invariant:
**The Voice Assistant CANNOT trigger emergency actions.**

Any command attempting to actuate emergency alerts, trigger sirens, or authorize evacuations is intercepted by `check_safety_violation()` and strictly returns `status: "REJECTED_SAFETY"`.

Response message returned to operator:
> *"Safety Rejection: Voice command cannot trigger emergency dispatches, sirens, or public alerts. Statutory authorization required from District Collector / DDMA under DMA 2005 (ENABLE_PUBLIC_DISPATCH=0)."*

## 2. Supported Intent Taxonomy
The Voice Assistant supports 14 grounded inquiry intents:

1. `INTENT_CORRIDOR_STATUS`: Overall status of a monitored corridor.
2. `INTENT_CRI_EXPLANATION`: Fused CRI score breakdown and mechanics.
3. `INTENT_FOS_EXPLANATION`: Geotechnical Factor of Safety and limit equilibrium.
4. `INTENT_RAINFALL_THRESHOLD`: Mandal-Sarkar empirical rainfall threshold comparison.
5. `INTENT_RISK_CHANGE`: Delta analysis comparing current and previous telemetry.
6. `INTENT_SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE`: Specific signals elevating slope risk.
7. `INTENT_CONTRADICTING_EVIDENCE`: Stabilizing factors and resistance forces.
8. `INTENT_MISSING_EVIDENCE`: Missing telemetry feeds or sensor coverage gaps.
9. `INTENT_AUTHORITY_RECOMMENDATION`: Protocol advisory for disaster management authority.
10. `INTENT_UNAVAILABLE_SOURCES`: Active fallback mechanisms and offline feeds.
11. `INTENT_SIMULATION_DISCLOSURE`: Disclosure of simulated vs real sensor telemetry.
12. `INTENT_WEATHER_FALLBACK`: Status of meteorological data sources and fallback modes.
13. `INTENT_ML_FALLBACK`: Model training status, dataset size, and limitations.
14. `INTENT_AI_SIREN_POLICY`: Institutional siren triggering policy under DMA 2005.

## 3. Grounded Synthesis Pipeline
All answers are dynamically synthesized from the `ExplanationContract` generated by `PahadExplanationEngine.get_corridor_explanation()`. Hallucinations are prevented by constraining the voice output directly to verified values:
- Exact corridor name, state, and district
- Exact CRI score, FoS value, and rainfall mm
- Exact corroboration state (`2/3 CORROBORATED`, etc.)
- Explicit provenance badges (`[LIVE]`, `[SIMULATED]`, `[HISTORICAL]`)
