## 2. EOC Command Dashboard Operational Architecture
The operational interface integrates 9 real-time intelligence feeds into a single command view without manual data-entry bottlenecks:
1. **PAHAD AI Real-Time Risk Overview**: Continuous telemetry fusion calculating Critical Risk Index (CRI: 0–100), Physical Infinite Slope Factor of Safety ($FoS$), and ML Event Probability ($P_{\text{event}}$).
2. **Active Incident Feed**: Real-time display of all active incidents across pilot sectors (NH-10 KM 48, NH-717A bypass, Tupul, Melthum).
3. **Alert Recommendations Awaiting Review**: Formal dossiers pending statutory District Magistrate or SDMA review.
4. **15 km Geodesic Population Exposure**: Immediate calculation of vulnerable populations across intersecting settlements.
5. **Road Network Connectivity & Detours**: Live status of NH-10 arterial corridor and BRO NH-717A bypass routes.
6. **IMD Meteorological Nowcasts**: 24h rainfall intensity, 72h Antecedent Precipitation Index (API), and radar reflectivity.
7. **NCS/USGS Seismic Telemetry**: Earthquake hypocenters and Peak Ground Acceleration (PGA) proxies.
8. **IoT Sensor & Edge Gateway Telemetry**: Health states of piezometers, borehole inclinometers, and LoRaWAN mesh concentrators.
9. **Multi-Channel Dispatch & Delivery Status**: Granular tracking across Web Push, Mobile Push, SMS (`[SIMULATED SMS]`), CAP v1.2, and Corridor Siren.

## 4. Canonical Incident Lifecycle State Machine
Incidents progress through a strictly controlled state machine. Unauthorized skips (such as `NEW` $\to$ `AUTHORIZED` without triage and review) are rejected:

```
[NEW] 
  │
  ▼
[TRIAGED] 
  │
  ├───────────────────────────────────┐
  ▼                                   ▼
[FIELD_VERIFICATION]          [AUTHORITY_REVIEW]
  │                                   ▲
  └───────────────────────────────────┘
                                      │
                                      ▼
                                [AUTHORIZED]
                                      │
                                      ▼
                                [DISPATCHED]
                                      │
                                      ▼
                              [ACKNOWLEDGED]
                                      │
                                      ▼
                                [MONITORING]
                                      │
                                      ▼
                                 [RESOLVED]
```
- **Alternative / Terminal States**: `REJECTED` (false positive with justification), `CANCELLED` (withdrawn advisory), `EXPIRED` (timeout escalated).

## 5. Multi-Criteria Queue Prioritization Algorithm (Checkpoint 8-04)
Incident queues in the EOC dashboard sort dynamically using a composite priority score $S_{\text{prio}} \in [0, 100]$:
$$S_{\text{prio}} = 0.30 \cdot \text{CRI} + 0.20 \cdot (P_{\text{event}} \cdot 100) + 0.15 \cdot R_{\text{crit}} + 0.15 \cdot \left(\frac{\text{Pop}}{10000} \cdot 100\right) + 0.10 \cdot (Q_{\text{data}} \cdot 100) + 0.10 \cdot U_{\text{time}}$$

Where:
- $\text{CRI}$: Critical Risk Index (0–100).
- $P_{\text{event}}$: Machine Learning event probability.
- $R_{\text{crit}}$: Road segment strategic criticality (NH-10 lifeline = 95.0).
- $\text{Pop}$: Population exposed inside 15 km geofence.
- $Q_{\text{data}}$: Feature completeness & freshness score (0.0–1.0).
- $U_{\text{time}}$: Elapsed time urgency penalty.

### Restrained Display Projection (Checkpoint 8-04)
To avoid cognitive clutter during critical emergencies, each incident entry projects five clean operational badges:
- **AI PREDICTION**: `Event Prob: 88.0% (CRITICAL)`

- **PROVENANCE**: `[SIMULATED] Telemetry + [LIVE] Open-Meteo`
