### 1. The Core Safety Doctrine

In critical mountain highway corridors such as Sikkim's National Highway 10, false alarms can induce civilian panic, trigger disorderly mass evacuations, block vital supply lines, and undermine public trust in civil protection authorities. Conversely, delayed warnings during extreme rainfall events can cost lives.

To resolve this operational tension, PARVAT NETRA enforces the **Core Safety Doctrine**:

$$\text{AI Early-Warning Recommendation} \neq \text{Public Emergency Alert}$$

Under this invariant, machine learning algorithms and physics models act exclusively as **evidence synthesis systems**. They never possess statutory authority to issue alerts directly to the public or trigger acoustic sirens.

### 4. Fail-Closed Review Timeout Governance

Alerts awaiting human review cannot be left open indefinitely without safety monitoring.
- **Timeout Threshold**: Configurable (default 900 seconds / 15 minutes).
- **Behavior on Expiry**:
  - `EXPIRE`: Fails closed directly to `STATE_EXPIRED`. The alert is withdrawn from operational queues and logged in the immutable audit chain.
  - `ESCALATE`: Elevates the review tier from `FIELD_OPERATOR` or `DISTRICT_AUTHORITY` to `STATE_AUTHORITY` (State Relief Commissioner / SDMA).
- **Safety Invariant**: Under no circumstances does a timeout auto-dispatch an alert. An unreviewed alert can NEVER transition to `STATE_WARNING_AUTHORIZED` or `STATE_PUBLIC_DISPATCH`.
