## 3. In-Depth Risk Analysis & Scientific Limitations

### 3.1 RSK-01: Limited Historical Event Sample Size
- **Description**: The GSI NLSM and state disaster archives provide 17 verified, documented mass movement events across the 8 NER states. Partitioned temporally:
  - Training: 16 samples (8 events, 8 defensible controls; $\le 2023$)
  - Validation: 12 samples (4 events, 8 controls; H1 2024)
  - Test: 8 samples (5 events, 3 controls; H2 2024)
- **Scientific Impact**: While test ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and CSI achieved $1.0$, this is a mathematical property of small held-out test sets ($N=8$). It does **NOT** guarantee zero real-world error.
- **Operational Rule**: The system must never claim production deployment readiness until multi-monsoon continuous telemetry ($N > 500$ events) is collected. Status remains **`TRAINED_LIMITED_DATA`**.

### 3.2 RSK-02: Absence of Production-Scale In-Situ Borehole Deployments
- **Description**: High-precision borehole inclinometers, vibrating-wire piezometers, and automated extensometers require multi-crore civil engineering installations. Currently, sensor feeds in corridors without physical hardware are generated by a validated physics-grounded simulator.
- **Scientific Impact**: Prediction outputs at corridors without live IoT hardware reflect physical-statistical estimates rather than live underground displacement.
- **Operational Rule**: Every UI card and API payload displays provenance badges (`[SIMULATED]`, `[LIVE / DETERMINISTIC]`).

### 3.3 RSK-03: InSAR Pipeline Latency & Earth Observation Cadence
- **Description**: Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellites like Sentinel-1 have a 6-to-12 day repeat orbit cycle. InSAR interferometry cannot provide real-time seconds-level landslide detection.
- **Scientific Impact**: Ground deformation velocity ($v_{\text{LOS}}$) functions as an antecedent hazard conditioning factor, not an immediate trigger.
- **Operational Rule**: Real-time triggering is handled by high-frequency rainfall gauges, seismometers, and in-situ tiltmeters, while InSAR defines spatial susceptibility polygons.

### 3.4 RSK-04: Modality Covariance in 2-of-3 Triangulation
- **Description**: The 2-of-3 rule requires confirmation across three pillars:
  1. Geotechnical Slope Equilibrium ($FoS < 1.05$)
  2. Precipitation Saturation ($R_{24h} > 120\text{ mm}$ or $API_{30d} > 250\text{ mm}$)
  3. Landslide Event Classifier ($P \ge 0.70$)
- **Scientific Impact**: In physical reality, $FoS$ is directly decreased by precipitation infiltrating the slip surface and elevating pore-water pressure. Thus, pillars (1) and (2) are physically covariant.
- **Operational Rule**: The EOC triage workflow requires human officers to inspect orthogonal non-covariant modalities: CCTV optical flow crack apertures, citizen ground reports, and seismic high-frequency tremors.

### 3.5 RSK-05: Siren Actuation & Autonomous Alert Liability
- **Description**: Accidental public siren actuation or false evacuation alerts in sensitive Himalayan border corridors (e.g., NH-10 Sikkim, Tawang Arunachal Pradesh) can trigger stampedes, economic disruption, and national security risks.
- **Operational Rule**: Autonomous AI dispatch is strictly forbidden by software and policy:
  - `SIREN_DRY_RUN=1`
  - `ENABLE_PUBLIC_DISPATCH=0`
  - `CAP_PRODUCTION_DISPATCH=0`
  - All public broadcasts require cryptographic dual-key authorization from the designated District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) Incident Commander.
